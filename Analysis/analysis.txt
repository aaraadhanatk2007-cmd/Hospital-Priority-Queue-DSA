COMPLEXITY COMPARISON

Heap Sort:
Best Case: O(n log n)
Average Case: O(n log n)
Worst Case: O(n log n)
Space: O(1)

Quick Sort:
Best Case: O(n log n)
Average Case: O(n log n)
Worst Case: O(n²)
Space: O(log n) average


ANALYSIS AND JUSTIFICATION

For the given input, Quick Sort performed fewer comparisons
and swaps than Heap Sort. However, these results are specific
to the given input.

For the hospital application, patients are continuously
inserted and the highest-priority patient is needed immediately.
A Max Heap is suitable because the highest severity score is
always at the root. Insertion takes O(log n) time and accessing
the highest-priority patient takes O(1) time.

Therefore, a Max Heap is suitable for the hospital priority
queue, while Heap Sort and Quick Sort are useful when the
complete set of severity scores needs to be sorted.
